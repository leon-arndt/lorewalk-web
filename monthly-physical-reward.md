# Physical reward: design brief

This brief is for the designer of the Lorewalk Step Tokens. It says what to design, what the factory can make, and what to deliver.

## The project

Lorewalk is a walking game for Singapore. A player walks to real landmarks, checks in, and collects cats that hatch from eggs. The game also counts each step, at any place.

A player who walks 200,000 steps in a month reaches the Monthly Step Goal. A count of such months earns a real metal coin: after 1, 3, 6, and 12 months, then every 6 months. The app calls the coin a Step Token, because "coins" is already the name of the money in the game.

The player collects the token in person at the Community Walk, a monthly event in Singapore. The token is the reason to subscribe, so it must feel worth the walk. It is a keepsake, not a prize from a race. A ribbon medal is too much for it.

## What to design

Design 6 faces: 5 fronts and 1 back.

- **Front for 1, 3, 6, and 12 months**: one front for each of the four counts.
- **Front for the later tokens**: one front for 18 months and up. It has a blank field for the count, and the supplier engraves 18, 24, 30, and so on by laser.
- **Back**: the same on all tokens. It makes the tokens one set.

Start with the back and the front for 1 month. The other four fronts follow after the approval of those two.

## The item

The token is a die-cast challenge coin with a design on both sides. These values come from the product page of Gift Market in Singapore, a possible supplier.

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Material | Die-cast metal alloy |
| Sides | A design on both sides |
| Diameter | 1.5, 1.75, or 2.0 inch (38, 44, or 51 mm). Not decided yet |
| Thickness | 3 mm |
| Color fill | None, or up to 6 flat colors |
| Finish | Gold, silver, copper, antique gold, antique silver, or antique copper |

- Make each design readable at 1.5 inch, the smallest size.
- Design for metal only: raised areas, recessed areas, and texture. An antique finish makes the recessed areas dark and the raised areas bright, so the relief does the work of contrast. Color fill is an option for later, not a base for the design.
- A different finish for each token is possible, and it needs no extra mould.

## The front

Each front must have:

- The count of months as the main element: 1, 3, 6, or 12. The front for the later tokens has the blank field in that place.
- One detail that sets it apart from the other fronts, so a player can tell the tokens apart without the number.

Each front can have:

- A sign of the walk, such as footsteps. The app draws a ring of small dots on the rim.
- A short word, such as "months", if it stays readable at 1.5 inch.
- A Singapore motif, or one of the cats from the game.

Do not put these on the front:

- A year or the name of a month. Each design stays in use for years, and Lorewalk orders it again when the stock is gone.
- A festival or a religious symbol.

The fronts must read as one family, and they must also read as steps. The token for 12 months must look like more than the token for 1 month.

## The back

The back never changes, so it holds only things that are true for each token.

- The Lorewalk name or mark.
- A flat, blank area for a serial number, such as "No. 042". The supplier engraves the number on each token by laser after the cast. The area needs room for 3 digits.
- No count, no month, and no year.

## The look

The app looks like a field journal: paper pages, ink outlines, red stamps, and postmarks. The token must look like it comes from the same world. Think of a heritage token or a postmark, not a sports medal.

The app already shows a simple drawing of each token. Open the app, go to the profile screen, and find "Step Tokens". These drawings are placeholders. Lorewalk draws them again from the final designs.

The metal has no color, but a mockup or a presentation sheet can use the colors of the app. The app gives one color to each token.

| Name | Hex | Use in the app |

| Paper | `#F5EEDC` | The page |

## What the factory can make

A die-cast coin cannot show everything that a drawing can.

- Use flat shapes at a small number of heights. Gradients and photo detail do not cast.
- Keep each line thick. A thin line fills in or breaks. The supplier gives the minimum line width on request.
- Keep text short and large. Small text becomes unreadable in metal.
- Keep text at least 1.5 mm from the edge.
- Use only fonts with a license for commercial use.

## What to deliver

1. Two or three sketch directions for the back and the front for 1 month.
2. One vector file for each approved face, as AI, EPS, or PDF. The supplier does not accept JPEG or PNG.
3. In each file: black and white artwork, text converted to outlines, and a note that marks which areas are raised, recessed, or textured.
4. One mockup of the front and the back at real size.

## Open points

Leon decides these before the work starts.

- The diameter.
- The finish of each token.
- The deadline and the fee.
- The file of the Lorewalk mark for the back.
- The minimum line width, from the supplier.
