## WIP

- **sound editor**: a VSCode extension that edits the game's sound events, in the vein of FMOD. `tools/vscode-soundbank/` holds the extension. It is plain JavaScript with no build step, and F5 starts it through `.vscode/launch.json`. It reads and writes `public/sounds/soundbank.json`, which `src/lib/sfx.ts` loads at runtime. A sound pack is a directory below `public/sounds/packs/` with a `pack.json` manifest. Three events exist so far: `ui/click`, `ui/close`, and `music/town-theme`. The background music runs through the bank as a `bus: "music"` event. Remaining work: add real events for check-in, hatch, and level-up, source more CC0 packs, and add a player-facing pack picker in Settings. The license of `town-theme.mp3` is not recorded. Confirm it before any public release.

- **news**: an in-app News overlay, opened from a button on the map HUD (bottom right, mirrors the weekly walk button). `NewsOverlay.tsx` renders the list and the post detail. `useNews.ts` reads the `news_posts` table when online and falls back to `src/data/news.ts` when offline, the same way `usePois` falls back to `singapore-pois.ts`. Post title and body are per-locale `jsonb`, with an English fallback. Nobody has applied the migration (`supabase/migrations/20260817000000_news_posts.sql`) or authored a real post yet.
